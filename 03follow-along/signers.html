Who were the signers?

Famous Signers

Among the 56 signers of the Declaration of Independence some stand out more than others. Some of the most famous signers are...

John Hancock
John Hancock, a Massachusetts native, is well remembered for bing the largest signature on the Declaration of Independence
Thomas Jefferson
Thomas Jefferson, a delegate from Virginia, was the principal author of the Declaration of Independence. He later served as the 3rd president of the United states and is remembered for his contributions to american ideals of liberty and democracy. Thomas Jefferson was the primary drafter of the Declaration of Independence. He died on July 4, 1826. (Information from The whitehouse's website)
George Taylor
George Taylor signed for Pennsylvania. He was the only one of the signers to be a former indentured servent. (Information from Daughters of the American Revolution's website.)
John Adams
John Adams, representing Massachusetts, was a vocal advocate for Independence in the continental congress. He became the 2nd president of the united states and played a key roll in drafting foundational documents and promoting the cause of freedom.
Benjamin Franklin
A well known scientist, scholar, diplomat, author, Benjamin Franklin was the oldest signer of both the Declaration of Independence and of the constitution. He founded the first public lending library in america and the first fire department in Pennsylvania. He is credited with inventing the Franklin stone, bifocal lenses and the lighning rod. He was responsable for negotiating the treaty of alliance with France in 1778. In 1783 he, John Adams and John Jay, traveled to France to sign the treaty of Paris and bring an offical end to the Revolutionary war. Even as early as the 1730s he was strongly against slavery and in 1787 he was elected the first president of the Pennsylvania Society for Promoting the Abolition of Slavery. (Information from Daughters of the American Revolution's website.)

There were many more influentcal people that signed the Declaration of Independence. These are just a few of the more popular ones.
