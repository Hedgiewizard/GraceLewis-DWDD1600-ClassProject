Dinner Takes All

Dinner Takes All is an "all-new cooking game show where two families compete to see whose Dinner Takes All.(BYUtv's Dinner Takes All main page )" Where the two families compete between cooking a family dinner and playing games with the host.s

My family was invited to be on Dinner Takes All by a family friend who works in the BYU media department. He mentioned they were doing a cooking show and asked if we wanted in. Obviously we did!

An important part of a cooking show is what did we make? My family made a classic Sunday dinner. We made seared tri-tip steak, siracha honey shrimp, potatoes with mushrooms and feta, brussel sprouts and chocolate chip cookies.

Unfortunately I don't think I can iframe the episode my family was on so I will just link that page here and put the trailer for the season my family is on below. Then if you and your family want to make the meal we made on the episode the different recipes will be linked below the video.
